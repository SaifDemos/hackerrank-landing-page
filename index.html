NOT JUST ANOTHER
TECH CLUB

We're a student-run technical club with committees across security, design, AI, development and the fundamentals. Each one runs hands-on sessions, talks and challenges that cover both the technical grind and the human side of the field.

HANDS-ON TRAINING

Real projects, real tools, real practice — not slideshows. Every committee builds things students can actually point to.

MANY COMMITTEES

Cybersecurity, UI/UX, AI/ML, backend, programming fundamentals and more — find the committee that fits where you want to grow.

COMMUNITY FIRST

Whether you're deep technical or brand new to the field, there's a committee, a team, and people happy to show you the ropes.
